00Executive Summary

Ronnie and Andrei aligned on the foundational problem facing Hivelocity's AI & data challenges: data is fragmented across systems, no one currently owns the strategy, and there is neither the internal budget nor the operational capacity to build solutions end-to-end.

Ronnie explicitly endorsed bringing in an outside AI/data specialist partner for a one-time consultative engagement to architect the foundation, with the internal team taking the keys to iterate from there. He acknowledged there is no internal owner of this initiative today and signaled openness to Andrei leading the planning phase.

Andrei's commitment: deliver a phased strategic plan within 1–2 weeks Due Mid-May 2026

01Critical Acknowledgments

  1. 01

    There is no internal budget or capacity — an outside specialist partner is required.

    Ronnie was direct: "We're going to have to outsource to get all that… we don't have the resources and we're not going to build the operational budget." His preferred engagement model is a one-time consultative build, not a managed service relationship — bring a specialist in with proper guidance to stand up the foundation, then the internal team iterates and evolves from there. This is an explicit green light to recommend an AI / data integration partner in the strategic plan.

  2. 02

    The data foundation is broken — and that's the starting point.

    Ronnie was unambiguous: "anything from an AI perspective is only as good as the data behind it." He sees the disparate-systems problem clearly — Ubersmith, Zendesk, AlertBridge, Zabbix, and QuickBooks Desktop (yes, you read that right) all running in isolation with no cross-functional layer. His team is currently performing physical inventory audits across our five core facilities and reconciling against Ubersmith and QuickBooks in Excel spreadsheets. That is, by his own description, the current state of truth at Hivelocity.

  3. 03

    There is no central owner of this initiative today — and there needs to be.

    Ronnie flagged this as one of the biggest gaps. In his words: "We need somebody that's going to kind of lead and own that aspect of it… here's this big pile. Organize the pile and figure out where we need to go next." Brandon Hall, Matt Tonic, Schoenbeck, and Javi's team all have ideas, but no one is holding the thread. He wants direction-setting ownership — not necessarily the execution engine for everything.

  4. 04

    Andrei is the natural internal fit to lead the planning.

    Matt Schatz (CRO) had already put Andrei's name forward before this meeting ("Matt said you're big into AI and he was talking about it when we were in Austin"). Ronnie's posture throughout was that if Andrei is willing to shape this, he wants a plan to react to. He opened the door directly: "depending on what you're looking to do, there's open opportunity."

02Strategic Context

Traditional DCIM is not the answer

Ronnie does not want to invest in packaged platforms like Sunbird or Hummingbird. His mental model: clean data architecture → LLM interface → query in plain English. "I just don't think you're going to need those systems anymore… it'll just be a natural language model like you see with GPT or Claude." Custom-built, voice-driven, report-on-demand.

Snowflake + BI tooling was premature

Ronnie believes leadership "jumped the gun way too quickly" on that investment. The data isn't clean enough yet for BI tooling to be meaningful. Important political signal: foundational data work has to land before any analytics-layer recommendations.

Financial data is the starting wedge

Ronnie and David Brolsma (CFO) are already scoping work on the financial side. Operational AI / data work follows. He is deliberately avoiding disruption to in-flight Zendesk / AlertBridge work right now to preserve momentum.

Pragmatic on AI agents

He's familiar with agentic concepts (referenced OpenClaw directly) and frames them as "cron jobs with a brain" — useful, but not hype-driven.

03Recommended Engagement Model

The picture that emerges from Ronnie's constraints and stated preferences:

  • Internal
    Architect & Owner     Andrei — owns the strategic plan, sets direction, holds the cross-functional thread, and manages the partner relationship. Note: Ronnie is not aware of Andrei's move to Llamassist at this time.
  • External
    Execution Muscle     A specialist AI / data partner engaged for a one-time foundation build (data architecture, integration layer, natural-language query interface), with the internal team taking ownership for iteration post-handoff.
  • Phased
    Rollout     Financial data first → operational data second → customer-facing AI capabilities third.

This model directly matches Ronnie's stated constraints (no operational budget for headcount, no appetite for ongoing managed services) and his stated preference for internal ownership of the long-term thread.
